# Help Centre

## Account & Billing

### How do I reset my password?
Go to Settings > Security > Change Password. Enter your current password and your new password. You'll receive a confirmation email.

### How do I upgrade my plan?
Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Select your new plan — changes take effect immediately. You'll be charged the prorated difference.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your account will remain active until the end of your current billing period. No refunds for unused time.

### Where can I find my invoices?
Go to Settings > Billing > Invoices. All invoices are available as PDF downloads. We also email them to the billing contact on file.

## Technical

### The app is slow. What can I do?
1. Check your internet connection
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Check our [status page](https://status.example.com) for known issues

### How do I export my data?
Go to Settings > Data > Export. Select the data types you want and the format (CSV or JSON). We'll email you a download link when it's ready.

### API rate limits
- Free plan: 100 requests/minute
- Pro plan: 1,000 requests/minute
- Enterprise plan: 10,000 requests/minute
