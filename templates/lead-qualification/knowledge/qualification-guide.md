# Lead Qualification Guide

## BANT+Fit Framework

### Budget (0-20 points)
- **20:** Has budget allocated, shared specific range
- **15:** Discussing budget internally
- **10:** Aware of pricing, no objection
- **5:** Unclear about budget
- **0:** No budget / price objection

### Authority (0-20 points)
- **20:** C-level or VP with purchasing authority
- **15:** Director or Head of Department
- **10:** Manager with influencing power
- **5:** Individual contributor
- **0:** No decision-making role

### Need (0-20 points)
- **20:** Clear pain point your product directly solves
- **15:** Recognised problem but exploring solutions
- **10:** Some interest, unclear need
- **5:** Vague interest
- **0:** No identifiable need

### Timeline (0-20 points)
- **20:** Plans to buy within 30 days
- **15:** Plans to buy within 1-3 months
- **10:** Plans to buy within 3-6 months
- **5:** No clear timeline
- **0:** Not planning to buy

### Company Fit (0-20 points)
- **20:** Exact ICP match (size, industry, tech stack)
- **15:** Strong overlap with ICP
- **10:** Partial ICP match
- **5:** Adjacent to ICP
- **0:** Outside ICP

## Tiers

| Tier | Score | Action |
|------|-------|--------|
| MQL | 75-100 | Schedule sales meeting |
| Warm | 50-74 | Add to nurture campaign, share case studies |
| Cold | 0-49 | Send overview materials, don't push |

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

- **Company size:** 50-500 employees
- **Industry:** Technology, SaaS, E-commerce
- **Role:** Engineering, Product, or Operations leadership
- **Tech stack:** Cloud-native, modern toolchain
- **Use case:** Workflow automation, developer tools, API products

## Discovery Questions

1. "What prompted you to look for a solution like ours?"
2. "How are you handling this today?"
3. "Who else on your team would be involved in this decision?"
4. "What's your timeline for making a change?"
5. "Do you have a budget range in mind for this?"
