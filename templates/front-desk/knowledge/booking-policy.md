# Hotel Policies & Information

## Check-in / Check-out
- **Check-in:** 3:00 PM
- **Check-out:** 11:00 AM
- Early check-in or late check-out may be available on request ($25 fee).

## Cancellation Policy
- Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in.
- 50% charge for cancellations within 24-48 hours.
- Full charge for cancellations within 24 hours or no-shows.

## Room Types
| Type | Max Guests | Rate/Night |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Standard | 2 | $129 |
| Deluxe | 3 | $199 |
| Suite | 4 | $299 |

## Amenities
- Free WiFi throughout the property
- Complimentary breakfast (7am–10am)
- Fitness center (24/7)
- Rooftop pool (8am–10pm)
- On-site parking ($15/day)

## Pet Policy
- Pets allowed in Standard and Deluxe rooms only.
- $40 pet fee per stay.
- Max 2 pets per room.

## FAQs
**Q: What time is breakfast?**
A: 7am to 10am daily in the Garden Room.

**Q: Do you have parking?**
A: Yes, on-site parking is $15 per day. No reservation needed.

**Q: Are you pet-friendly?**
A: Yes! Pets are welcome in Standard and Deluxe rooms. $40 fee per stay, max 2 pets.

**Q: Can I get an extra bed?**
A: Rollaway beds are available for Deluxe and Suite rooms ($20/night).
